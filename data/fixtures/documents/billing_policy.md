# Ardleigh Consulting Group: Client Billing Policy

Fictional document, created for a demonstration. Version 6, approved by the Finance Committee, March 2024.

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out how client fees are recorded, adjusted and invoiced across the Group.

## 2. Billing basis

Fees are billed on the agreed basis in the engagement letter: time and materials, fixed fee or milestone. Agreed client rate cards take priority over standard rates.

## 3. Work in progress

Time and expenses are posted to the project in the period they are incurred. Project managers review work in progress every month.

## 4. Billing adjustments

### 4.1 Write-ups

A write-up increases the billable value of work in progress. The engagement partner must record the reason.

### 4.2 Write-downs

A write-down reduces the billable value of work in progress. A write-down of more than 15% of the billable value of the billing line requires the prior approval of the engagement partner. Write-downs at or below this level may be applied by the project manager.

### 4.3 Records

Approvals must be retained for seven years, with the name of the approver and the date of approval.

## 5. Closed projects

A project may not be closed while it holds unbilled work in progress. Any remaining balance must be billed or written off first.

## 6. Review

This policy is reviewed every two years by the Finance Committee.
