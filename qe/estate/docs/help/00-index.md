## Conventions in this guide

- Screen labels, buttons and fields are written in **bold**, exactly as they appear on screen.
- Messages from Project Billing are written in quotation marks, exactly as they appear.
- Amounts are written with a full stop for decimals and a comma for thousands, for example 1,050.00 EUR. This is also how Project Billing shows them.
- Kestrel Consulting Oy's company currency is the euro (EUR). Rates, adjustments and limits are always held in EUR, even when the customer is invoiced in another currency.

## Who does what

Most billing work is done by the billing clerks. Billing managers look after customer settings, holds and exchange rates. Project managers check proposals for their projects and mark fixed-price milestones as complete. Ask your team lead if you are not sure which tasks are yours.
