# 28. Glossary

Last updated: June 2025

**Adjustment.** A write-up or write-down on a proposal line, entered in EUR with a reason.

**Agreed rate.** An hourly rate for a role agreed in a customer's framework agreement. It takes priority over the project rate card and the standard rate.

**Agreement discount.** A percentage set on the customer, from 0 to 50, taken off time lines only.

**Billable.** A flag on a time entry or expense. Only billable entries are invoiced.

**Billing hold.** A setting on a customer or project that stops new proposals. A reason is required.

**Billing run.** The monthly round of creating, checking and finalising proposals.

**Business date.** The fixed date Project Billing works to, shown in the top bar. Set by the finance systems team.

**Closing.** A project status. The minimum invoice amount does not apply, so final small amounts can be billed.

**Company currency.** EUR. Rates, expenses, milestones, adjustments and limits are held in EUR.

**Credit note.** A document that reduces a final invoice. Numbered CN-000001 and onwards.

**Deferred.** The status of a proposal worth less than the minimum invoice amount. Its entries stay unbilled.

**Due date.** The invoice date plus the customer's payment terms in days, or 30 days if the terms are 0.

**Exchange rate.** The amount of the customer's currency that one euro buys, effective from a date.

**Expense markup.** A percentage added to the cost of expenses on a project.

**Final.** The status of an invoice or credit note that has been numbered and posted. It cannot be changed.

**Finalise.** To turn a proposal into a numbered, final invoice.

**Fixed price.** A project billed by milestones. Its hours are not billed.

**Invoice date.** The date on the invoice. It decides the exchange rate and starts the payment terms.

**Milestone.** A fixed amount on a fixed-price project, billed once when it is complete.

**Minimum invoice amount.** 50.00 EUR before tax. Smaller proposals are deferred unless the project is closing.

**Not-to-exceed limit.** The most that can be billed on a project, in EUR before tax, counting earlier invoices less credit notes.

**Payment terms.** The number of days a customer has to pay, set on the customer.

**Period end.** The last date of the work included in a proposal.

**Proposal.** A draft invoice for one project. Referenced as P00001 and onwards.

**Purchase order (PO).** The customer's order number. Required on invoices for some customers, 4 to 30 characters.

**Quarter-hour billing.** Each time entry is rounded up to the next 0.25 hours before pricing.

**Rate card.** A project's own hourly rates by role.

**Reserved.** Time and expenses on an open proposal. No other proposal can pick them up until the proposal is deleted.

**Reverse charge.** No VAT on services to business customers in another EU country with a VAT number. Tax code RC.

**Rounding.** Each line is converted and rounded on its own. The total is the sum of the rounded lines.

**Standard rate.** The default hourly rate for a role.

**Tax code.** A code on each line that sets the VAT rate, taken from the project.

**Time and materials.** A project billed by approved hours at hourly rates, plus expenses.

**Write-down.** A negative adjustment that reduces a line. It cannot take the line below zero.

**Write-up.** A positive adjustment that increases a line.
